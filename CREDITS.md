# Credits & Asset Attributions — NusaQuest

NusaQuest is a proposal prototype built for **BAIFEST 2026**. All graphical assets used in this game are source-free, open-license, and cleared for non-commercial and commercial distribution.

## Tile Assets

1. **Kenney Roguelike RPG Pack**
   - **Source**: [Kenney.nl — Roguelike Pack](https://kenney.nl/assets/roguelike-rpg-pack) / [GitHub Mirror](https://github.com/ETdoFresh/kenney.nl/tree/master/roguelike-pack)
   - **Creator**: Kenney (Asset Jesus)
   - **License**: [CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain)](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
   - **Used For**: Base grass tiles, dirt paths, water pond tiles, fences, trees, and sawah crop tiles (`assets/tiles/kenney_roguelike.png`).

2. **Kenney RPG Pack**
   - **Source**: [Kenney.nl — RPG Pack](https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-base) / [GitHub Mirror](https://github.com/ETdoFresh/kenney.nl/tree/master/kenneyrpgpack)
   - **Creator**: Kenney
   - **License**: [CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain)](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
   - **Used For**: Market stall structures, crates, and village decor (`assets/tiles/kenney_rpg.png`).

3. **Kenney RPG Urban Pack**
   - **Source**: [Kenney.nl — RPG Urban Pack](https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-urban-pack) / [GitHub Mirror](https://github.com/ETdoFresh/kenney.nl/tree/master/kenney_rpgurbanpack)
